02 / The approach

The prototype is part of the point.

I work across product design, front-end systems, game mechanics, and world-building. The common thread is a bias toward making the idea executable: give it a surface, a rule, a small feedback loop, and see what it teaches us.

Every card links to its source repository. When there is a public build, it is linked too. The archive is intentionally broad; unfinished things are still allowed to be useful.
